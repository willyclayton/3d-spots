Exploring Giza

Explore

  • Drag to turn · right-drag or two fingers to pan
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom · W A S D to pan
  • Double-click or double-tap to fly to a spot
  • Tap a label or open Places

Fly

  • W A S D to fly, Q / E down and up, Shift faster
  • Drag to look · scroll to glide
  • Touch: joystick, drag to look, ▲ ▼ to climb

Walk

  • W A S D to walk, Shift to run
  • Click to look with the mouse, Esc to release
  • Touch: joystick, drag to look

Keys

  • O explore · G fly · V walk
  • C cutaway · X x-ray · B era
  • T tour · L labels · H help

Terrain from SRTM elevation data. Dimensions follow Petrie’s survey and later work; some chamber positions in Khafre and Menkaure, and the shape of the 2017 “Big Void”, are approximate.
